Wood Logs

Step into the embrace of contemporary sophistication and rustic charm with our Wood Log-themed room at Casa Concreto. This meticulously designed space is a testament to the seamless fusion of nature-inspired aesthetics and modern luxury, offering guests a retreat that is both inviting and stylish. The room’s focal point is the exquisite wood log furniture, carefully selected to create a cozy and inviting atmosphere. Each piece, from the bed frame to the seating area, tells a story of craftsmanship and natural beauty.

The grandeur extends into the bathroom, where a single stone-carved bathtub awaits. A skylight above the bathtub invites you to gaze at the stars, transforming your evening bath into a mesmerizing celestial experience. In daylight, the bathroom becomes a sunlit haven, illuminating the stone elements and creating an atmosphere of tranquillity.
