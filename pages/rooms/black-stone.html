Black Stone

Step into the Black Stone-themed space at Casa Concreto, where encaustic elegance and moody allure create a world of captivating design. This room is defined by its encaustic floor tiles, meticulously crafted from concrete, forming an intricate mosaic that graces the entire room—a visual tapestry that exudes elegance and artistry.

Furnishings, adorned with jute robes, echo a design philosophy that seamlessly marries contemporary luxury with the organic charm of nature. The use of jute robes not only adds a tactile and inviting quality but also introduces sustainability, aligning with the natural theme of the space. This harmonious integration of furniture into the room’s design ensures that every moment spent here is not just luxurious but also immersed in the cozy embrace of well-crafted, nature-inspired pieces.

A distinctive feature of the Black Stone room is the bathroom’s garden view, providing a unique perspective during your shower. This intentional design choice connects the indoor space with the natural beauty outside, creating a harmonious blend of modern luxury and the tranquility of nature. A singular black stone washbasin becomes a statement piece, adding a touch of opulence to the space.
