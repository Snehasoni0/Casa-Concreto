Policies & House Rules

Yes, we are pet-friendly and provide ample room for your furry friends to roam. However, pets are strictly not allowed in the swimming pool or kitchen areas.

To maintain a peaceful environment, all-male groups are not allowed, and we request guests keep noise to a minimum after 10 PM. Smoking is permitted only in exterior areas.

The property is equipped with CCTV cameras in external areas and fire extinguishers for your safety. Additionally, our staff remains on the premises 24/7.

Stay & Amenities

Every room is designed for comfort with a king-sized bed, a private deck offering panoramic countryside views, and a dedicated conversation area.

Our professional chef team prepares fresh, home-cooked meals. Guests can enjoy a wide choice from our a la carte menu or book special experiences like a floating breakfast in the pool.

Guests have access to an all-weather pool, a home theatre system, a pool table, and board games. For outdoor activities, we offer cricket, badminton, football, and bicycles.
