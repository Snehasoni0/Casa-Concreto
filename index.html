ALL FOR YOU

Nestled at the end of the gated Prieta Estates neighborhood within Peninsula Papagayo, Villa Avellana redefines ultra-luxury hospitality. The privacy of a single-family home, the scale of a five-star hotel and the highly personalized concierge service of a private club combine to offer a best-in-class destination in a spectacular setting for all ages and interests.

The fully-dedicated staff of twenty anticipates and caters to guests’ every need, from private chefs to adventure guides to beach safety advisors. Cruise around on the 42' yacht, take a private yoga class or SUP right in Prieta Bay. Everything is included in your stay.
